How the cycle works

1. Join a tier and lock the required collateral plus the P5 entry fee. 2. Wait in the large tier queue. 3. Eligible participants can commit in queue order. A participant who does not commit is skipped; a participant who commits and then misses the deadline has the contribution taken from collateral. 4. Ten funders contribute the tier contribution. 5. You receive the tier payout. 6. You move to the back and can participate again. 7. If a reserved funder misses the deadline, their collateral covers the missed contribution and they must restore collateral before becoming eligible again.
