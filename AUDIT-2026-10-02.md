## Security observations
- Production authentication is fail-closed when JWT secret is absent.
- Production CORS now fails startup if CORS_ORIGIN is absent.
- Wallet ownership is proved cryptographically by signed challenge.
- Wallet challenge expires after 10 minutes and is single-use.
- Reconciliation discrepancy resolution now requires an operator note.
- Database queries are parameterized throughout the reviewed server paths.
- `npm install` currently reports 45 vulnerabilities in the dependency tree, including 7 high-severity findings. These must be dependency-audited and resolved deliberately; `npm audit fix --force` was not used blindly.

## Current conclusion
The active BNB branch is materially cleaner and safer than the earlier mixed Stellar/BNB state. The financial Testnet path is proven and the Stage 3 reconciliation/operations layer is running. The remaining blockers are primarily production security, compliance, custody, external provider integrations, dependency remediation, and independent contract audit rather than another blockchain rewrite.
